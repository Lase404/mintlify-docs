## What is Direkt?

Direkt is a crypto payment infrastructure API. It generates one-time deposit addresses for your users, monitors incoming on-chain transfers, and gives you real-time status updates — all without you ever holding funds.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a quote">
    POST to `/api/quote` with the amount, currency, and destination chain. Direkt returns a unique deposit address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show the address to your user">
    Display the address (and QR code) in your UI. The user sends crypto to it from any wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Poll for status">
    GET `/api/status/:id` until the status is `confirmed` or `completed`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fulfil the order">
    Once confirmed, release the goods or service to the user.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key concepts

- **No prefunding** — You never lock up capital. Funds go directly from sender to your wallet.
- **Non-custodial** — Direkt never holds your money.
- **One-time addresses** — Every payment gets a fresh address, keeping transactions private and traceable.
- **Webhook-ready** — Poll the status endpoint or wire up a webhook for push notifications.
